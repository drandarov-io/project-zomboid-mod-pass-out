version=1
title=Pass out
description=[h1]Informations:[/h1]
description=
description=Causes your character to fall asleep on the spot if you have max tiredness for X in-game hours (default 6). The amount of hours and whether a warning is given, is configurable in the sandbox options.
description=This is intended for a more hardcore playthrough.
description=
description=[h1]Features:[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Compatible with [b]Build 42[/b] and [b]Build 41[/b]
description=[*] Compatible with [b]Singleplayer[/b] and [b]Multiplayer[/b] (with MP instant-wake fix and split-screen support)
description=[*] [b]Traits:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Prone to Passing Out[/b] (+4 pts): More prone to collapsing (20% faster countdown, faster blackout fade, panic save drains faster)
description=[*] [b]Resistant to Passing Out[/b] (-4 pts): Resists passing out longer (20% slower countdown, longer blackout fade, panic save holds longer)
description=[/list]
description=[*] [b]Collapse Animation (B42):[/b] Sinks to the ground when passing out instead of standing asleep upright, and stands back up on waking
description=[*] [b]Vehicle Support:[/b] Drop at the wheel: car rolls on and you slump onto the horn, drawing nearby zombies
description=[*] [b]Full Sandbox Options:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Master on/off toggle
description=[*] Pass Out Hours & Randomness
description=[*] Required Tiredness Level (Exhausted, Very Tired, or Tired)
description=[*] Passing Out Minutes (fade duration)
description=[*] Pass Out Warning toggle (private Halo text)
description=[*] Panic Keeps You Awake toggle
description=[*] Forced Pass-Out Duration (locks early wake-by-moving in MP)
description=[*] Pass-Out Sleep Quality (Floor, Rough, Average, Good)
description=[*] Collapse Animation toggle
description=[*] Pass Out While Driving toggle
description=[/list]
description=[*] [b]Project Viewpoint[/b] compatibility overlay
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Notes:[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Periodic checks run every in-game minute
description=[*] Any panic can keep you awake while active, with panic draining each minute until you drop
description=[*] Eating enough vitamins or coffee to drop tiredness below threshold resets the countdown
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Known issues:[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] None so far
